How to read this: Score = performance percentile − (Pay weight × salary percentile), computed within same position and season. Positive means outperforming their pay grade (underpaid); negative means overpaid relative to position peers. Performance percentile comes from a weighted composite — 12 stats for batters (xwOBA, xBA, xSLG, Barrel%, Hard-Hit%, Chase%, Whiff%, K%, BB%, OAA, FRV, DRS), 13 for pitchers (xERA, xBA/Avg Exit Velo/Hard-Hit%/Barrel% Allowed, K%, BB%, Chase%/Whiff%/Z-Swing% Induced, Extension, Stuff+, Location+) — each stat scored as a z-score against the position average. Adjust any weight, including Pay, in the Weights tab and every score, grade, and salary projection on the site recomputes instantly. Minimum sample: 150 PA for batters, 50 IP for pitchers.

Pre-arbitration players skip the pay adjustment — by CBA rule they're paid at or near the league minimum almost regardless of performance, so weighing their salary would just penalize them for a number they don't control. Their Score is pure performance percentile, and they're broken out in the Rookies tab with a projected market salary based on comparable-performing market-priced peers (Arbitration, Extension, or Free Agent deals) at their position.

▲/▼ arrows next to a player's name (and on their card) flag a gap between their actual AVG/ERA this season and a projected true-talent level, blending two independent signals: a Marcel-style projection (their own last 3 seasons of expected stats, regressed to the league mean by sample size and adjusted for age) and a comparable-player projection (historical players — 2015–2024 — who were at the same age with a similar recent performance level and trend, weighted by how those comps actually performed the following season). Both are real, established statistical techniques, not black-box models. Green ▲ means they're underperforming that projection and due for positive regression; red ▼ means they're running ahead of it and likely to cool off. For pre-arb players, this projection also nudges their projected salary so a small-sample hot or cold streak doesn't fully drive the number.

Weights

These weights are the site's single source of truth — every score, grade, and projected salary on Home, Players, Rookies, Teams, and the player card is computed live from them. Each stat is measured as a z-score against the position average, multiplied by its weight, and summed into a raw performance subtotal. That subtotal is converted to a percentile within the position, then Pay below blends in how well-paid the player is: Score = performance percentile − (Pay weight × salary percentile). Pre-arbitration players are paid near the league minimum by rule, so they skip the pay adjustment entirely and their Score is just their performance percentile.
