How to read this dashboard How to read this: Score = bWAR percentile − (Pay weight × salary percentile). Performance is what a player actually produced: Baseball-Reference WAR, ranked against all hitters (position-adjusted by WAR itself), all starters, or all relievers — playing time counts, so missing time lowers it. Salary percentile is within the player’s own position. Positive means outperforming their pay grade (underpaid); negative means overpaid. A separate Predicted Score swaps actual bWAR for the bWAR their repeatable process stats (xwOBA, xERA, barrel rate, K%, …) say they should have produced; the gap between the two separates sustainable production from luck. Adjust the predicted-score weights, or Pay, in the Weights tab. Minimum sample: 150 PA for batters, 50 IP for starters, 25 IP for relievers.

Pre-arbitration players skip the pay adjustment — by CBA rule they're paid at or near the league minimum almost regardless of performance, so weighing their salary would just penalize them for a number they don't control. Their Score is pure performance percentile, and they're broken out in the Pre-Arbitration tab with a projected market salary based on comparable-performing market-priced peers (Arbitration, Extension, or Free Agent deals) at their position.

▲/▼ arrows next to a player's name (and on their card) flag a gap between their actual AVG/ERA this season and a projected true-talent level, blending two independent signals: a Marcel-style projection (their own last 3 seasons of expected stats, regressed to the league mean by sample size and adjusted for age) and a comparable-player projection (historical players — 2015–2024 — who were at the same age with a similar recent performance level and trend, weighted by how those comps actually performed the following season). Both are real, established statistical techniques, not black-box models. Green ▲ means they're underperforming that projection and due for positive regression; red ▼ means they're running ahead of it and likely to cool off. For pre-arb players, this projection also nudges their projected salary so a small-sample hot or cold streak doesn't fully drive the number.

Weights

The site’s Score ranks what a player actually produced: Baseball-Reference WAR, against all hitters, all starters, or all relievers. The weights below don’t change that — they drive the Predicted Score: expected bWAR, i.e. what a player’s repeatable process stats say their bWAR should have been given the playing time and position they had. Each weight is bWAR per full season (600 PA / 100 IP) for a one-standard-deviation-better stat, and the defaults were fit by non-negative least squares on every completed season 2015–2025 (they explain about — of hitter WAR and — of pitcher WAR out of sample). A stat at 0 adds nothing once the others are in (e.g. xSLG is already inside xwOBA). Both scores subtract Pay: Score = bWAR percentile − (Pay weight × salary percentile), with salary percentile within the player’s position. Pre-arbitration players are paid near the league minimum by rule, so they skip the pay adjustment. Weights you change here are saved in this browser.
